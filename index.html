FAQ

Does it work on every site?

No. It supports certain direct media files and unprotected streams. DRM-protected and otherwise restricted content is not supported.

Are my files uploaded?

Conversion is designed to run locally on your device. The extension may request media resources from the websites you visit.

Can I buy Pro now?

Not yet. The subscription and license service is being prepared.
